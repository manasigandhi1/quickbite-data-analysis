🍔 Quick Bite Data Analysis
📊 Project Overview
Analyzed a multi-city food delivery platform to identify key drivers of revenue, profitability, and operational efficiency. The project transforms raw transactional data into actionable business insights.

🎯 Business Objective
  Identify high-value customers and revenue drivers
  Evaluate city, restaurant, and driver performance
  Improve profitability and operational efficiency
🗂️ Dataset
  ~6000 orders, 1800+ customers across 5 cities
  Tables: Customers, Orders, Restaurants, Drivers
  Key fields: revenue, profit, age group, segment, ratings
🛠️ Tools Used
  SQL
  Excel
  Tableau
🧹 Data Preparation
  Standardized city names
  Handled missing driver ratings
  Removed duplicates and validated revenue
🔍 Key Insights
  Age 31–45 drives highest revenue (core target segment)
  Premium & Regular users generate most profit despite equal distribution
  Revenue drops after age 45 (low engagement segment)
  City profits are similar (no dominant market)
  Few restaurants dominate revenue (high dependency risk)
  Driver efficiency is moderate (optimization opportunity)
  Seasonality impacts revenue (March & November dips)
📈 Dashboard

Built an interactive dashboard tracking:
