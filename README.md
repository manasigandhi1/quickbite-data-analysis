# 🍔 Quick Bite Data Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

Developed an interactive data analysis project for a multi-city food delivery platform to evaluate revenue performance, customer behavior, restaurant contribution, and operational efficiency.

The project transforms transactional order data into actionable business insights that help identify growth opportunities, profitability drivers, and operational improvement areas.

The analysis focuses on customer segmentation, city-wise performance, restaurant dependency, and delivery operations.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem / Objective

The food delivery platform needed better visibility into customer behavior, revenue distribution, and operational performance across multiple cities.

The business wanted to understand:

- Which customer groups generate the highest revenue and profit?
- Which cities and restaurants contribute most to business performance?
- Are operations running efficiently across delivery drivers?
- What seasonal trends affect revenue and orders?
- Which business areas require optimization for growth and profitability?

This project was designed to support data-driven decision-making through interactive analytics and business insights.

## 🔍 Key Insights

- Customers aged **31–45** contribute the highest revenue and represent the core business segment
- Premium and Regular users generate the majority of total profit despite balanced customer distribution
- Revenue contribution declines significantly after age 45, indicating lower engagement among older users
- Profit contribution across cities remains relatively balanced, with no single dominant market
- A small number of restaurants contribute a large share of total revenue, creating dependency risk
- Driver efficiency is moderate, highlighting opportunities for operational optimization
- Seasonal revenue fluctuations are visible, with noticeable declines during March and November
- Customer segmentation analysis highlights strong monetization potential among high-engagement users

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

- Focus marketing campaigns on the high-value 31–45 age group
- Increase Premium plan conversions through targeted loyalty programs
- Improve engagement strategies for older customer segments
- Reduce dependency on a small number of restaurants by expanding partnerships
- Optimize driver allocation and delivery operations to improve efficiency
- Plan promotional campaigns around low-performing seasonal periods
- Monitor customer retention and repeat-order behavior for long-term growth
